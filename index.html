Top three activities to do in Cape Town

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach is located just before arriving at Camp's Bay and is perfect for relaxing.

Surfing at Muizenberg

Enjoy the sun and learn to surf with world-class surfing water.

Hike up Table Mountain

Challenge yourself and attack the hills of the famous Table Mountain. If you only want to enjoy the view, take the quick tram up.
